# Sound sources

All recordings below are from the Red Library sound-effects collection on the Internet Archive, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication). They were recorded for Hollywood studios in the 1930s–40s; the age and noise are part of why they were chosen.

| File | Original title | Collection |
|---|---|---|
| footsteps-tile.mp3 | R10-05 Footsteps on Hard Tile Floor | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Footsteps_1 |
| steps-quick.mp3 | R10-14 Quick Steps on Wooden Floor | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Footsteps_1 |
| children-laughing.mp3 | R16-05 Children Laughing | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Voices_Children |
| recess.mp3 | R08-15 School Kids at Recess | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Crowds_Children |
| hushed.mp3 | R08-39 Children Move and Speak in Hushed Tones | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Crowds_Children |
| sliding-door.mp3 | R09-05 Sliding Door | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Doors |
| sliding-door-slam.mp3 | R09-17 Sliding Door Slam | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Doors |
| door-rattle.mp3 | R29-49 Rattling Door Open and Close | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Doors |
| door-squeak.mp3 | R09-16 Long Door Squeak | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Creaks |
| wooden-chair.mp3 | R17-41 Wooden Chair | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Creaks |
| chimes.mp3 | R04-40 Department Store Chimes | https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Bells_Horns_Whistles |

Everything else (the fluorescent hum, the school bell, chalk, the ball, crickets, frogs, the far scooter, the stingers) is synthesised in `audio.js`.
